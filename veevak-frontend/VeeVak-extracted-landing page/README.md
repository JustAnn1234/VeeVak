💔 THE PROBLEM (Human + Real)
Section Title
Small businesses work hard — but operate blindly
Body Copy
Across Nigeria and other emerging markets, many small businesses run entirely through messaging apps and physical interactions.
Orders, prices, customer details, and payments live in:
WhatsApp and Instagram chats
Facebook and TikTok messages
Voice notes and screenshots
Notebooks and memory
As a result, many business owners don’t know:
What actually sells best
How much they earn weekly or monthly
Which platform brings the most value
Whether their business is growing or quietly declining
This lack of visibility leads to stress, poor decisions, and business failure — even when demand exists.
🔍 WHY EXISTING TOOLS DON’T WORK
Section Title
Why current solutions fall short
Body Copy
Most existing business tools are built for formal companies with websites, staff, and structured systems.
For informal sellers:
Accounting software is too complex
POS systems only work for physical stores
Ecommerce platforms require full online shops
Platform tools are locked to a single app
These tools don’t reflect how small businesses actually operate today — across chats, platforms, and physical sales.
💡 THE SOLUTION
Section Title
Clarity without complexity
Body Copy
VeeVak is an AI-powered operations assistant designed specifically for informal and small businesses.
With user permission, VeeVak:
Extracts sales data from chat conversations
Allows simple logging of offline or physical sales
Organizes this information into clear summaries
Business owners can see:
Revenue over time
Best-selling products
Repeat customers
Performance across sales channels
No spreadsheets.
No accounting jargon.
Just clarity.
🤖 WHY AI (RESPONSIBLE USE)
Section Title
Why AI is necessary — and used responsibly
Body Copy
Sales conversations are unstructured, informal, and scattered across platforms. Manually tracking this data is time-consuming and error-prone.
AI allows VeeVak to:
Understand informal language
Extract meaning from conversations
Turn scattered information into structured insights
VeeVak uses AI responsibly:
Only with user consent
Focused on assistance, not surveillance
Designed to support better decisions, not replace human judgment
🚀 HOW VEEVAK GROWS (VISION)
Section Title
From sales visibility to business health
Body Copy
VeeVak starts by helping businesses understand their sales.
As data becomes structured, VeeVak will expand into:
Financial health alerts
Cash flow warnings
Early business risk signals
This expansion — called BizSentry — helps small businesses act early, not when it’s already too late.
